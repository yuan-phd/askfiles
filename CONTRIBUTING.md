# Contributing

Thank you for your interest in AskFiles.

AskFiles is licensed under the GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 only (see [LICENSE](LICENSE)). The author also wants to be able to offer the project under other terms, for example a commercial license outside the AGPL. For that, every contribution has to come with a contributor agreement that lets the author license the contribution under other terms as well as under the AGPL.

That agreement does not exist yet. Until it is published here, pull requests with code, documentation or other content cannot be accepted.

You are welcome to open an [issue](https://github.com/yuan-phd/askfiles/issues) to report a problem or suggest a change. Do not paste content from confidential documents.
