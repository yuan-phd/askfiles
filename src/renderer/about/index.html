AskFiles answers questions from your own documents. Everything runs on this Mac: your documents and your questions never leave it.

Built on open source

  • AnythingLLM by Mintplex Labs Inc., MIT License. The document search and answering engine runs as the AskFiles backend.
  • Ollama, MIT License. Runs the language models on this Mac.
  • Electron, MIT License, with Chromium and Node.js. The application shell.
  • Tesseract language data (English, Simplified Chinese), Apache License 2.0. Text recognition in scanned documents.
