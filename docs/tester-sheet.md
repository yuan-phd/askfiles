# AskFiles Test: tester sheet / 测试说明

Thank you for testing AskFiles. This test version keeps a record of your questions and of what you click, on your computer only. Nothing is uploaded. At the end you decide whether to send the record.

感谢您测试 AskFiles。这个测试版只在您的电脑上记录您提的问题和您的点击操作，不会上传任何内容。测试结束时，由您决定是否发送这份记录。

## English

**Before you install**

- Asking questions needs a computer with at least 16 GB of memory. With 8 GB, only Browse and Find work.
- The first start downloads the models: about 8.5 GB with 16 GB of memory, about 10.4 GB with 32 GB or more (1.2 GB with 8 GB). It needs an internet connection and can take tens of minutes.
- Keep at least 15 GB of free disk space.

**1. Install**

- Mac: open AskFiles Test.dmg and drag AskFiles Test to Applications, then open it. If the Mac refuses, open System Settings, Privacy & Security, and click Open Anyway. On older macOS versions, right-click AskFiles Test in Applications and choose Open instead. If it still will not open, open Terminal, run the line below, then open the app again.

  ```
  xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/AskFiles Test.app"
  ```
- Work computers managed by your company may block the app. If so, use a personal computer.
- Windows: double-click AskFiles-Test-Setup.exe. If a blue window says Windows protected your PC, click More info, then Run anyway.
- On first start a short notice explains the record. Click Continue.

**2. What to try**

- Use your own documents: Add files, Add folder, or drag them into the window. Use documents you know well, in English or Chinese.
- Ask questions the way you would ask a colleague. Some about one fact, some naming a document ("what does the travel policy say about taxis?"), some comparing two documents. A question comparing two documents takes about a minute longer than others; the app is not stuck.
- Read the answer and its sources. Click a source to read the section, or to show the file.
- Sentences marked † have no word-for-word support in the sources. They may be a summary or conclusion drawn from the sources, or they may be inaccurate. Check them against the source cards. Tell us whether such a sentence was right or wrong.
- Use Browse: the library, a document's outline, Find, Compare.
- When an answer looks wrong or strange, write down the question and why it seems wrong to you (for example: the document says 30 days, not 14). We cannot see your documents, so your note is the only way we can tell what went wrong.

**3. Export and send the record**

- Open Settings: on a Mac, AskFiles Test menu, Settings, or Cmd+,; on Windows, the AskFiles menu at the top of the window, Settings, or Ctrl+,. Find Test record and click Export test record.
- Choose **Questions with answers and sources**. It holds your questions, the answers and the documents and sections they cite, which we need to understand your notes. (Questions only leaves out the answers and document names; use it only if you are not comfortable sharing those.)
- The answers may contain text from your documents. You can open the file and read it before sending.
- The file is saved on your Desktop and shown in Finder or File Explorer. Send the file and your notes together, by email or a shared folder.
- In Settings you can also pause recording at any time, or clear the record.

**4. When the test ends**

The person who invited you will send you the regular AskFiles. Install it: it keeps your libraries and models and deletes the test record. On a Mac, also move AskFiles Test to the Trash.

## 中文

**安装之前**

- 提问需要至少 16 GB 内存的电脑。8 GB 内存的电脑只能使用 Browse（浏览）和 Find（查找）。
- 首次启动会下载模型：16 GB 内存约 8.5 GB，32 GB 或以上约 10.4 GB（8 GB 内存为 1.2 GB）。需要联网，可能要几十分钟。
- 请保留至少 15 GB 的可用磁盘空间。

**1. 安装**

- Mac：打开 AskFiles Test.dmg，把 AskFiles Test 拖到“应用程序”，然后打开它。如果被阻止，请打开“系统设置”中的“隐私与安全性”，点按“仍要打开”。在较旧版本的 macOS 上，请改为在“应用程序”里右键点按 AskFiles Test，选择“打开”。如果仍然无法打开，请打开“终端”，运行下面这一行，再重新打开应用。

  ```
  xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/AskFiles Test.app"
  ```
- 由公司管理的工作电脑可能会阻止这个应用。如果遇到这种情况，请使用个人电脑。
- Windows：双击 AskFiles-Test-Setup.exe。如果出现蓝色窗口“Windows 已保护你的电脑”，请点击“更多信息”，再点击“仍要运行”。
- 首次启动时会显示一段关于记录的简短说明，点击“继续”即可。

**2. 可以试试**

- 使用您自己的文档：Add files（添加文件）、Add folder（添加文件夹），或直接拖进窗口。请用您熟悉的中文或英文文档。
- 像问同事一样提问：有的问一个具体事实，有的提到某份文档（例如“差旅制度对打车有什么规定？”），有的比较两份文档。比较两份文档的问题比其他问题多用约一分钟，应用并没有卡住。
- 阅读回答和来源。点击来源可以阅读对应章节，或在文件夹中显示文件。
- 带 † 标记的句子在来源中没有逐字对应的原文，可能是根据来源归纳的结论，也可能不准确，请对照来源卡片核实。请告诉我们这样的句子是对是错。
- 使用 Browse（浏览）：文档库、文档大纲、Find（查找）、Compare（比较）。
- 当回答看起来有误或奇怪时，请记下这个问题，以及您认为它哪里不对（例如：文件写的是 30 天，不是 14 天）。我们看不到您的文档，您的记录是我们了解问题所在的唯一途径。

**3. 导出并发送记录**

- 打开设置：Mac 上点 AskFiles Test 菜单中的 Settings，或按 Cmd+,；Windows 上点窗口顶部 AskFiles 菜单中的 Settings，或按 Ctrl+,。找到“测试记录”（Test record），点击“导出测试记录”（Export test record）。如果您的电脑语言是英文，这些按钮会以英文显示。
- 请选择 **“问题以及回答和来源”**（Questions with answers and sources）。它包含您的问题、回答以及回答引用的文档和章节，我们需要这些内容来理解您的记录。（“只含问题”（Questions only）不含回答和文档名称；只有在您不愿分享这些内容时才选择它。）
- 回答中可能包含您文档里的文字。发送之前，您可以打开文件看一看。
- 文件会保存到桌面，并在访达或文件资源管理器中显示。请把这个文件和您的记录一起，通过邮件或共享文件夹发送。
- 在设置中，您也可以随时暂停记录或清除记录。

**4. 测试结束后**

邀请您参加测试的人会把正式版 AskFiles 发给您。安装后，它会保留您的文档库和模型，并删除测试记录。在 Mac 上，请再把 AskFiles Test 移到废纸篓。
